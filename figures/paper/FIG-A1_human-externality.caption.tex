Effect of the agentic working set on the human class, at 30\% agentic share with volumes and cache fixed. (a) Human p99 latency rises from 76.1 to 113.0 ms (+36.9 $\pm$ 1.0 ms, t = 36.3). (b) The human miss ratio changes by a factor of 1.06 (0.232 to 0.247). (c) Origin load rises from 36.8 to 52.7 req/s. Mean of 5 runs; whiskers $\pm$ 1 SE, smaller than the markers where not visible. The upstream channel through which origin load reaches human latency was not measured.
