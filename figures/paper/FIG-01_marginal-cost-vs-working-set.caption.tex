Marginal origin cost of the agentic class at three working-set sizes. In each row the agentic class is raised from 12 to 36 req/s (13\% to 30\% of load) while the human and exhaustive classes stay fixed at 55 and 28 req/s; only the scope from which agentic sessions are drawn changes. Rows give the nominal working set (scope $\times$ corpus $\times$ 3 contiguous chapters) as a multiple of the measured cache capacity of 5,274 objects. Bars: mean of 5 runs; whiskers: $\pm$ 1 SE, smaller than the bar ends. The values are marginal between 12 and 36 req/s; in the first row the first 12 req/s cost +0.103 origin requests each and the net effect from 0 to 36 req/s is positive (+0.392 $\pm$ 0.158). The tag marks the row closest to real agents by windowed working set (distinct objects within 143 s: honeypot 90th percentile 670, about 693 for that row), not by nominal size.
