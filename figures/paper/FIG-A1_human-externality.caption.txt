Effect of the agentic reachable set on the human class, at 30% agentic share with volumes and cache fixed. (a) Human p99 latency rises from 72.9 to 99.6 ms (+26.7 ± 1.3 ms, t = 20.3). (b) The human miss ratio changes by a factor of 1.07 (0.231 to 0.247). (c) Origin load rises from 37.4 to 52.4 req/s. Mean of 5 runs; whiskers ± 1 SE, smaller than the markers where not visible. The upstream channel through which origin load reaches human latency was not measured.
