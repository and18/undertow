Calibration of the synthetic agentic class against agent-class clients observed on the theslowshelf.org honeypot (12 Aug – 21 Sep 2026). (a) Distinct objects requested within a 143 s window, the characteristic time of the testbed cache: median 4, 90th percentile 670 and maximum 678 across 31 clients (mean 240), against about 693 for the lowest testbed setting. (b) Share of agent sessions that read three contiguous chapters: 4.3% observed across 1,929 sessions, against 100% assumed by the generator. One honeypot, one population and one period; the comparison does not characterise agentic traffic on the Web in general.
