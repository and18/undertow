Calibration of the synthetic agentic class against agent-class clients observed on a honeypot on a public measurement site (12 Aug – 21 Sep 2026, UTC). (a) Distinct objects requested within a 143 s window, the characteristic time of the testbed cache: median 4, 90th percentile 670 and maximum 678 across 31 clients (mean 240), against about 693 for the lowest testbed setting. (b) Contiguity, with the same metric on both sides: the share of consecutive request pairs within one session that read adjacent chapters of the same book. On the honeypot a session is a TCP connection: 4.25% of 1,929 pairs (284 connections, 37 clients), and 19.4% repeat the same chapter. On the generator a session is a k6 virtual user: 66.9% of 7,169 pairs (mean of 5 seeds), 0% repeated: two of the three chapters of a session follow a contiguous one, while the pair between two sessions almost never does. One honeypot, one population and one period; the comparison does not characterise agentic traffic on the Web in general.
